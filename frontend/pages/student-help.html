Frequently asked questions

Can I join more than one queue?

You can be in one queue at a time. Joining another queue leaves your current one.

How do I know when it's my turn?

You'll get a notification when you're third, second and next in line, and again when your ticket is called.

What happens if I miss my turn?

Your ticket is marked skipped and you'll need to rejoin the queue at the back.

Can I leave a queue?

Yes. Use Leave Queue on the Dashboard or My Queue page; your ticket is cancelled.
